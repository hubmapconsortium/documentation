summary: A multiplexed, high-resolution fluorescence imaging technique that quantifies the spatial distribution of dozens of proteins within intact cells and tissues. In each imaging cycle, the specimen is stained with fluorescently labeled antibodies, imaged, and then chemically eluted, allowing sequential rounds of staining to build a comprehensive protein map. This iterative approach enables simultaneous spatial profiling of hundreds of protein targets at subcellular resolution within a single tissue section.
